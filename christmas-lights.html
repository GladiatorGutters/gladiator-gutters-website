Christmas Lights Installation

Holiday Lighting, Hung Safely and Taken Down On Schedule

Professional installation, timer setup and January takedown — so your roofline looks great over the holidays and nobody in your family has to climb a ladder.

Why hire it out

The same crew that hangs your gutters, hangs your lights

Climbing a ladder onto a roof edge in December isn't worth the risk of a fall or a cracked gutter from an over-tightened clip. Our crew installs commercial-grade C9 and mini string lights along your roofline, gutters and eaves using gutter-safe clips — no nails, no screws, no damage to your seamless system.

We handle the whole season: install before Thanksgiving, a mid-season bulb check if you'd like one, and takedown in January so the lights don't become a spring eyesore. Ask about seasonal storage, and we'll box everything up and store it until next year.
